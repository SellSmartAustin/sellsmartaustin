Before You Reach Out

Straight answers to the usual questions.

Do I need to repair or clean the property?
No. You can sell the home in its current condition — no repairs, cleaning, or staging. Foundation, roof, plumbing, mold, tenants, probate: we buy it in whatever condition it's in today, and contents can stay.
How is the cash price determined?
It's based on real Austin-area comparable sales, the home's condition, and the cost of the work it needs — not a random lowball figure. Because a licensed Texas Realtor® is on our team, we can also show you what the same home might net if listed, so you can compare honestly.
Is requesting an offer an obligation?
No. The offer is free, in writing, and yours to say no to. If the number's too low, tell us. If none of the options fit, that's a completely acceptable outcome — no cost, no pressure.
